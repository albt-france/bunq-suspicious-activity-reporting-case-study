# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AML** | Anti-money laundering: the rules and controls that stop criminal money from entering or moving through the financial system. |
| **CFT** | Countering the financing of terrorism. In French: LCB-FT (*lutte contre le blanchiment de capitaux et le financement du terrorisme*). |
| **CDD** | Customer due diligence: identifying and verifying the customer and the beneficial owner, and understanding the purpose of the relationship. |
| **EDD** | Enhanced due diligence: deeper checks when the risk is higher (more documents, source of funds, closer monitoring). |
| **FIU** | Financial intelligence unit: the national body that receives and analyses suspicious-activity reports. France: **Tracfin**; Netherlands: FIU-Nederland. |
| **STR / SAR** | Suspicious transaction report / suspicious activity report. In France: **déclaration de soupçon (DS)**, sent to Tracfin through **ERMES**. |
| **Déclaration de soupçon** | French STR. Confidential: the customer must not be told (see `REPORTING_PROCEDURE.md` §5). |
| **Tipping-off** | Telling the customer or a third party that a report was made or an analysis is under way: prohibited. |
| **AMLR** | Regulation (EU) 2024/1624, the EU anti-money-laundering rulebook; applies from 10 July 2027. |
| **AMLA** | The EU Anti-Money Laundering Authority; prepares technical standards (e.g. the report format) and guidelines. |
| **CAP / CAP item** | Corrective action plan: the list of actions agreed after an audit, review or incident, each with an owner, a due date and evidence of closure. |
| **Gap analysis** | Requirement → current process → gap → action → priority → status. |
| **Alert / case / report** | An alert is a signal (rule, referral, request); a case is an investigation of one or more alerts; a report is what goes to the FIU. |
| **Precision (of a rule)** | Share of flagged accounts that are truly suspicious. Low precision = many false alerts to review. |
| **Recall (of a rule)** | Share of truly suspicious accounts that the rule flags. Low recall = missed cases. |
| **Typologies** | Recurring laundering patterns: **fan-out** (one account pays many), **fan-in** (many pay one), **structuring** (splitting amounts just under a threshold), **pass-through / layering** (money in and out quickly), **cycle** (money returns to its origin through several accounts), **scatter-gather / gather-scatter**, **mule account**. |
| **Difference-in-differences** | Effect of a change = (treated after − before) − (control after − before). Removes trends shared by both groups. |
